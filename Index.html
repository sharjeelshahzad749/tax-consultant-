5M Tax helps individuals and businesses effectively manage their tax obligations, bookkeeping, and Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) matters. We provide practical, reliable, and personalized support designed to make tax and accounting processes simpler and more manageable.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with clients outside the GTA?

Yes — services are available online for clients anywhere, alongside in-person appointments for local clients.

What do I need to bring to my consultation?

Prior year tax returns, income slips (T4/T4A/T5, etc.), and any relevant business or expense records help make the first session most productive.

Do you handle CRA audits and reviews?

Yes — 5M Tax provides direct support and representation for CRA matters, audits, and correspondence.

Is my free consultation really free?

Yes, the initial consultation to review your situation and outline next steps has no cost or obligation.
